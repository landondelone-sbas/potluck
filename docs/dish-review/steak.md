# Dish review sheet: whole-cut steak

Status: drafted on 2026-11-05. Not yet cooked, not yet reviewed.

Same use as the earlier sheets ([batch 1](batch-1.md), [2](batch-2.md), [3](batch-3.md)). These three dishes accept only whole-cut steak (steak, ribeye, NY strip, sirloin, filet mignon, flank, skirt, porterhouse, T-bone, tri-tip), which is where doneness matters. Steak sliced into tacos, noodles or pasta still goes to the older beef dishes. See [decision record 0007](../decisions/0007-steak-cuts.md).

**Food safety:** the USDA minimum for whole cuts of beef is 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest. The steps say so, and say that a lower doneness is the cook's choice and carries more risk. They do not recommend a lower temperature. Please read the live USDA page before launch.

## The three dishes


### Seared Ribeye with Asparagus

`steakdinner` · Comfort · 25 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ribeye, veg asparagus, spice black pepper, sauce mustard, starch baby potatoes.

**Accepts:** protein: steak; veg: asparagus, greenbeans, broccoli, mushroom, onion, greens, tomato, pepper, zucchini; spice: pepper, garlic, herbs, smoky, paprika, chili; sauce: mustard, gravy, cream, bbq, pesto; starch: potato, sweetpotato, bread, rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Take the ribeye out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead, pat very dry and season well with the black pepper and salt.
2. Sear in a very hot, lightly oiled pan 3 to 4 minutes a side for a 1-inch cut, then check the thickest part with a thermometer. The USDA safe minimum for steak is 145°F / 63°C. A lower doneness is your choice and carries more risk.
3. Rest the ribeye 3 to 5 minutes on a clean plate while you cook the asparagus in the same pan.
4. Slice against the grain and serve with the baby potatoes and the mustard.

**Lighter tip:** Make the veg half the plate, keep the steak to a palm-size portion and use mustard or herbs instead of a butter sauce.

### Sirloin Steak & Potato Skillet

`steakpotato` · Comfort · 30 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein sirloin steak, veg onions, spice garlic, sauce mustard, starch baby potatoes.

**Accepts:** protein: steak; veg: onion, pepper, mushroom, greens, broccoli, zucchini; spice: pepper, garlic, herbs, paprika, smoky; sauce: mustard, gravy, bbq, cream, hot; starch: potato, sweetpotato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Dice the baby potatoes small and pan-fry in oil for 12 to 15 minutes, until golden and tender, then move to a clean plate.
2. Pat the sirloin steak dry, season with the garlic and salt, and sear 3 to 4 minutes a side. Check the thickest part with a thermometer: the USDA safe minimum for steak is 145°F / 63°C. A lower doneness is your choice and carries more risk.
3. Rest the sirloin steak 3 to 5 minutes, then slice, and cook the onions in the same pan until soft.
4. Toss everything together with the mustard and serve hot.

**Lighter tip:** Use more veg than potato, slice the steak thin so a little goes a long way, and go light on the sauce.

**The same pot also offers:** Steak Salad with Onions; Seared Sirloin Steak with Onions.

- [ ] Does the toss at the end work with mustard, gravy, barbecue sauce, cream or hot sauce?

### Steak Salad with Arugula

`steaksalad` · Comfort · 25 minutes · Light

**Cooked from this pot:** protein flank steak, veg arugula, spice black pepper, sauce balsamic vinaigrette, starch crusty bread.

**Accepts:** protein: steak; veg: greens, tomato, cucumber, onion, pepper, avocado, mushroom; spice: pepper, garlic, herbs, chili, smoky; sauce: vinaigrette, mustard, ranch, pesto; starch: bread, potato, quinoa.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Pat the flank steak dry, season with the black pepper and salt, and sear in a very hot pan 3 to 4 minutes a side. Check the thickest part with a thermometer: the USDA safe minimum for steak is 145°F / 63°C. A lower doneness is your choice and carries more risk.
2. Rest the flank steak 3 to 5 minutes on a clean plate, then slice thin against the grain.
3. Toss the arugula with the balsamic vinaigrette and pile onto plates.
4. Top with the sliced steak and serve with the crusty bread.

**Lighter tip:** Make the salad mostly veg, use a light dressing, and slice the steak thin so a small piece goes a long way.

**The same pot also offers:** Flank Steak Pita Wrap; Seared Flank Steak with Arugula.
