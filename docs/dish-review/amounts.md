# Amounts review sheet

For the owner's cooking review. Every amount is for **2 servings**, in metric, and the app converts it to US units and scales it for other servings. A count (no unit) is a number of whole items: eggs, tortillas, slices of bread, potatoes.

Each dish has **one amount per category**. When your pot holds two items of a category, they split it equally. Amounts are a guide, so mark any that would not feed two people or would not fit the pan.
