## X Paprikash with Y (`paprikash`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Creamy
- **Source:** ENGLISH - Boston.gov, page 21: Aunt Elizabeth's Chicken Paprikash (Boston Community Cookbook).
- **What changed from the source:** Served over noodles that are cooked separately, as in the source. Rice and potatoes also work. The source cooks the chicken five minutes. The app says until 165°F. The sour cream, wine, lemon and tomato paste are kept, with the sour cream as the sauce pick.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg pepper, onion, mushroom, tomato, greens; spice paprika, pepper, garlic; sauce cream, tomato, gravy; starch noodles, pasta, rice, potato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken breast (340 g); veg: bell peppers (250 g); spice: paprika (10 ml); sauce: sour cream (150 ml); starch: egg noodles (200 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Chicken Breast Paprikash with Bell Peppers):
1. Cook the egg noodles following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. Cut the chicken breast and the bell peppers into bite-size pieces. Cook in a hot pan with a little butter and oil, about 5 minutes, until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C).
3. Stir in the paprika for a minute. Add a splash of wine or broth, a squeeze of lemon and a spoonful of tomato paste, and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Turn the heat low and stir in the sour cream. Warm gently for 3 minutes without boiling, then spoon over the egg noodles.

**With Chicken thighs, Mushrooms, Jasmine rice, Alfredo** (Chicken Thighs Paprikash with Mushrooms):
1. Cook the jasmine rice following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. Cut the chicken thighs and the mushrooms into bite-size pieces. Cook in a hot pan with a little butter and oil, about 5 minutes, until cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C).
3. Stir in the paprika for a minute. Add a splash of wine or broth, a squeeze of lemon and a spoonful of tomato paste, and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Turn the heat low and stir in the alfredo. Warm gently for 3 minutes without boiling, then spoon over the jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Use plain Greek yogurt in place of sour cream, and double the peppers.

## X and Y Quinoa Skillet (`chickenquinoa`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Mediterranean | **Time:** 40 min | **Feel:** Balanced
- **Source:** Heart Healthy Recipe Guide, HFSA, page 23: Healthy Chicken Broccoli Quinoa.
- **What changed from the source:** One skillet, as in the source. The starch is quinoa only, because the 15-minute simmer is for quinoa. The vegetables are a slot, so the broccoli can be other vegetables.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg broccoli, onion, celery, greens, mushroom, carrot, zucchini; spice herbs, chili, garlic, pepper, lemon; sauce gravy, vinaigrette; starch quinoa
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken breast (340 g); veg: broccoli (250 g); spice: thyme (5 ml); sauce: chicken broth (480 ml); starch: quinoa (150 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Chicken Breast and Broccoli Quinoa Skillet):
1. Cut the chicken breast into 1-inch pieces, season with salt and pepper, and cook in a hot oiled skillet until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C). Move to a plate.
2. Cook the broccoli in the same pan for 3 to 4 minutes until softened, then stir in the thyme for a minute.
3. Add the quinoa and the chicken broth, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low about 15 minutes, until most of the liquid is absorbed.
4. Return the chicken breast, cover, and cook 5 more minutes until everything is tender and the liquid is gone.
5. Finish with a squeeze of lemon and serve from the pan.

**With Chicken breast, Spinach, Quinoa, Italian herbs** (Chicken Breast and Spinach Quinoa Skillet):
1. Cut the chicken breast into 1-inch pieces, season with salt and pepper, and cook in a hot oiled skillet until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C). Move to a plate.
2. Cook the spinach in the same pan for 3 to 4 minutes until softened, then stir in the italian herbs for a minute.
3. Add the quinoa and the chicken broth, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low about 15 minutes, until most of the liquid is absorbed.
4. Return the chicken breast, cover, and cook 5 more minutes until everything is tender and the liquid is gone.
5. Finish with a squeeze of lemon and serve from the pan.

**Lighter tip:** Pile in extra vegetables. They steam on top of the quinoa, and the pan stays one-dish.

## Slow-Cooked X with Y (`potroast`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 150 min | **Feel:** Slow
- **Source:** Heart Healthy Recipe Guide, HFSA, Complete Meals and Main Dishes: Slow-Cooked Pot Roast.
- **What changed from the source:** Baked in the oven for about 2 hours, as in the source, with no slow cooker needed. Only a roast (beef, pork or lamb roast, brisket, short ribs) is accepted, so ground beef or a steak never gets a pot roast. The source gives no temperature. The app says the roast is safe at 145°F and tender well past that.
- **Accepts:** protein roast; veg carrot, onion, celery, mushroom; spice pepper, garlic, herbs, smoky; sauce gravy; starch potato, sweetpotato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: beef roast (700 g); veg: carrots (300 g); spice: black pepper (5 ml); sauce: beef broth (240 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** protein:roast

**With its own defaults** (Slow-Cooked Beef Roast with Carrots):
1. Heat the oven to 325°F (160°C). Pat the beef roast dry and let it come toward room temperature while the oven heats.
2. Sear the beef roast in a heavy ovenproof pot with a little oil, 2 to 3 minutes per side, until evenly browned. Season with salt and the black pepper.
3. Scatter the carrots and the cut-up baby potatoes around the beef roast and pour in the beef broth. Cover tightly.
4. Bake about 2 hours, until very tender. It is safe once cooked through (beef roast 145°F / 63°C), and a roast is well past that by the time it is tender.
5. Rest the beef roast 15 minutes under foil, slice thinly, and serve with the vegetables and the pan juices.

**With Pork shoulder roast, Onions, Sweet potato, Gravy** (Slow-Cooked Pork Shoulder Roast with Onions):
1. Heat the oven to 325°F (160°C). Pat the pork shoulder roast dry and let it come toward room temperature while the oven heats.
2. Sear the pork shoulder roast in a heavy ovenproof pot with a little oil, 2 to 3 minutes per side, until evenly browned. Season with salt and the black pepper.
3. Scatter the onions and the cut-up sweet potato around the pork shoulder roast and pour in the gravy. Cover tightly.
4. Bake about 2 hours, until very tender. It is safe once cooked through (pork shoulder roast 145°F / 63°C), and a roast is well past that by the time it is tender.
5. Rest the pork shoulder roast 15 minutes under foil, slice thinly, and serve with the vegetables and the pan juices.

**Lighter tip:** Trim the visible fat and fill the pot with vegetables. The pan juices become a light gravy.

## Reverse-Seared X with Y (`reversesear`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 60 min | **Feel:** Rich
- **Source:** Allen Brothers: How To Reverse Sear A Steak.
- **What changed from the source:** The source pulls the steak at 110-130°F and finishes it in a pan, with final rested temperatures from rare to medium-well. The app keeps the USDA 145°F, says to pull at about 130°F for that, and says a lower doneness is the cook's choice and carries more risk. A baking potato (russet, Idaho, baked) is baked first at 425°F, the classic steakhouse pairing. Any other starch is cooked on the stove.
- **Accepts:** protein steak; veg asparagus, greenbeans, mushroom, broccoli; spice pepper, garlic, herbs; sauce cream, mustard, gravy; starch bakingpotato, potato, sweetpotato, rice, bread
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: NY strip (700 g); veg: asparagus (250 g); spice: garlic and rosemary (5 ml); sauce: herb butter (30 ml); starch: baked potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** starch:bakingpotato

**With its own defaults** (Reverse-Seared NY Strip with Asparagus):
1. Scrub the baked potatoes, prick with a fork, and bake at 425°F (220°C) for 45 to 60 minutes. Then lower the oven to 250°F (120°C). Pat the ny strip very dry and season well with salt.
2. Set the ny strip on a rack over a baking sheet and bake until the center is about 15°F under the finish you want, usually 25 to 45 minutes. Check with a thermometer. The USDA safe minimum for steak is 145°F / 63°C, so for that finish pull the steaks at about 130°F (54°C). A lower doneness is your choice and carries more risk.
3. While the steaks bake, cook the asparagus on the stove, any way you like: steam, sauté or boil.
4. Heat a heavy pan until smoking and sear the ny strip 1 minute on the first side. Flip, add the herb butter and the garlic and rosemary, and baste for about a minute.
5. Sear the edges briefly, then rest the ny strip 5 to 7 minutes and slice against the grain. Serve with the asparagus and baked potatoes.

**With Filet mignon, Mushrooms, Sweet potato, Ranch** (Reverse-Seared Filet Mignon with Mushrooms):
1. Heat the oven to 250°F (120°C). Pat the filet mignon very dry and season well with salt.
2. Set the filet mignon on a rack over a baking sheet and bake until the center is about 15°F under the finish you want, usually 25 to 45 minutes. Check with a thermometer. The USDA safe minimum for steak is 145°F / 63°C, so for that finish pull the steaks at about 130°F (54°C). A lower doneness is your choice and carries more risk.
3. While the steaks bake, cook the mushrooms on the stove, any way you like: steam, sauté or boil. Cook the sweet potato the way you like it too.
4. Heat a heavy pan until smoking and sear the filet mignon 1 minute on the first side. Flip, add the ranch and the garlic and rosemary, and baste for about a minute.
5. Sear the edges briefly, then rest the filet mignon 5 to 7 minutes and slice against the grain. Serve with the mushrooms and sweet potato.

**Lighter tip:** Keep the steak to a palm-size portion, and put the vegetable on half the plate.

## Orange-Glazed X with Y (`glazedbreast`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 75 min | **Feel:** Balanced
- **Source:** Heart Healthy Recipe Guide, HFSA, page 24: Orange Glazed-Turkey with Potatoes and Carrots.
- **What changed from the source:** Takes a turkey or chicken breast, never ground meat. The source gives no temperature, so the app says until 165°F (74°C) for poultry. Marmalade and jam count as the honey-type sauce.
- **Accepts:** protein breast; veg carrot, onion, celery, greenbeans, broccoli, zucchini; spice herbs, garlic, pepper; sauce honey; starch potato, sweetpotato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: turkey breast (680 g); veg: carrots (250 g); spice: mixed herbs (5 ml); sauce: orange marmalade (45 ml); starch: potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Orange-Glazed Turkey Breast with Carrots):
1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly oil a baking dish and set the turkey breast in the middle.
2. Rub the turkey breast with half the mixed herbs, salt and pepper, then spread the orange marmalade over the top.
3. Toss the carrots and the potatoes, cut into 2-inch chunks, with a little oil and the rest of the seasoning, and arrange around the turkey breast.
4. Bake uncovered 40 to 60 minutes, depending on size, until cooked through (turkey breast 165°F / 74°C) and the vegetables are tender.
5. Rest the turkey breast 5 to 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the vegetables.

**With Chicken breast, Green beans, Sweet potato, Honey** (Orange-Glazed Chicken Breast with Green Beans):
1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly oil a baking dish and set the chicken breast in the middle.
2. Rub the chicken breast with half the mixed herbs, salt and pepper, then spread the honey over the top.
3. Toss the green beans and the sweet potato, cut into 2-inch chunks, with a little oil and the rest of the seasoning, and arrange around the chicken breast.
4. Bake uncovered 40 to 60 minutes, depending on size, until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C) and the vegetables are tender.
5. Rest the chicken breast 5 to 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the vegetables.

**Lighter tip:** A sugar-free marmalade gives the glaze with far less sugar, and the vegetables roast in the same dish.
