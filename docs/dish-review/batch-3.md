## The eight dishes


### Ground Beef Chili

`chili` · Comfort · 50 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ground beef, veg onions, spice chili powder, sauce crushed tomatoes, starch cornbread.

**Accepts:** protein: beef, beans, turkey, sausage, pork, chicken; veg: onion, pepper, tomato, corn, celery, carrot, zucchini; spice: chili, cumin, paprika, smoky, garlic, pepper; sauce: tomato, hot, gravy; starch: rice, bread, potato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Brown the ground beef in a heavy pot, breaking it up, then add the onions and the chili powder and cook 5 minutes.
2. Add the crushed tomatoes and enough water or stock to cover. Canned beans only need draining; dried kidney beans need soaking, then a hard boil of at least 10 minutes, first.
3. Simmer 30 to 40 minutes, stirring now and then, until the chili thickens and any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.
4. Serve with the cornbread and something fresh, like onion, cilantro or lime.

**Lighter tip:** Use more beans and veg than meat, choose lean meat, and keep the cornbread to a small piece.

**The same pot also offers:** Ground Beef & Onions Tagine; Ground Beef & Onions Island Curry.

- [ ] The kidney bean warning is in step 2 for all pots. Does it read well when the beans are canned?
- [ ] Is 30 to 40 minutes enough for the meat to be at least 165°F and for the chili to thicken?

### Chicken Thighs & Carrots Noodle Soup

`chickensoup` · Comfort · 40 minutes · Cozy

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken thighs, veg carrots, spice italian herbs, sauce chicken broth, starch egg noodles.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, turkey, beans, sausage; veg: carrot, celery, onion, greens, mushroom, zucchini; spice: herbs, pepper, garlic, lemon; sauce: gravy; starch: noodles, pasta, rice, potato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Soften the carrots with the italian herbs in a large pot with a little oil.
2. Add the chicken broth, the chicken thighs and enough water to cover, then simmer 20 to 25 minutes, until any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.
3. Lift out any whole pieces of meat, shred them and return them to the pot.
4. Stir in the egg noodles, simmer until just tender, and finish with lemon or fresh herbs.

**Lighter tip:** Load it with veg, use plenty of broth and fewer noodles, and keep the salt low.

- [ ] Does 20 to 25 minutes cook raw chicken thighs through?

### Ground Beef & Carrots Potato Pie

`cottagepie` · Comfort · 60 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ground beef, veg carrots, spice thyme, sauce gravy, starch baby potatoes.

**Accepts:** protein: ground, beans; veg: carrot, onion, greenbeans, celery, mushroom, tomato, greens; spice: herbs, pepper, garlic, paprika; sauce: gravy, tomato; starch: potato, sweetpotato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Boil the baby potatoes until tender, then drain and mash with a little oil or milk.
2. Brown the ground beef with the thyme, then add the carrots and cook until soft.
3. Stir in the gravy and simmer 10 minutes, then spread in a baking dish and top with the mash.
4. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until golden and bubbling and the filling is at least 165°F / 74°C.

**Lighter tip:** Use lean meat or lentils, lots of veg in the filling, and mash with a little milk instead of butter.

**The same pot also offers:** Hearty Ground Beef & Carrots Stew; Ground Beef & Carrots Soup.

### Loaded Potato with Black Beans & Broccoli

`loadedpotato` · Comfort · 60 minutes · Hands-off

**Cooked from this pot:** protein black beans, veg broccoli, spice black pepper, sauce cheese, starch baked potatoes.

**Accepts:** protein: beans, sausage, pork, chicken, egg, beef, turkey; veg: broccoli, onion, pepper, tomato, mushroom, greens; spice: pepper, garlic, paprika, smoky, herbs, chili; sauce: cream, ranch, bbq, hot, buffalo, gravy; starch: potato, sweetpotato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C), scrub the baked potatoes, prick with a fork and bake 45 to 60 minutes, until soft.
2. Meanwhile cook the black beans with the black pepper until heated through, scrambling any eggs until fully set, then keep on a clean plate.
3. Sauté or steam the broccoli until tender.
4. Split the baked potatoes, fluff the insides and pile on the black beans, the broccoli and the cheese.

**Lighter tip:** Beans and plenty of veg on top, and a spoonful of yogurt in place of sour cream, make it a balanced meal.

**The same pot also offers:** Black Beans & Broccoli Skillet Hash.

### Kung Pao Chicken Breast

`kungpao` · Asian · 25 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg bell peppers, spice chili flakes, sauce peanut sauce, starch jasmine rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, shrimp, tofu, pork, beef; veg: pepper, zucchini, onion, greenbeans, carrot, mushroom, celery; spice: chili, ginger, garlic; sauce: peanut, soy, hot, honey, teriyaki; starch: rice, noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-size pieces and stir-fry in a very hot pan until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then move to a clean plate.
2. Stir-fry the bell peppers with the chili flakes for 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Return the chicken breast, add the peanut sauce and toss until glossy.
4. Serve over the jasmine rice, topped with chopped peanuts if you have them.

**Lighter tip:** Use more veg than meat and a small amount of sauce, and skip the extra sugar.

### Chicken Breast & Bok Choy Broth Noodles

`brothnoodles` · Asian · 25 minutes · Cozy

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg bok choy, spice ginger, sauce chicken broth, starch ramen noodles.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, egg, pork, tofu, shrimp, beef; veg: greens, mushroom, carrot, cabbage, onion, greenbeans; spice: ginger, garlic, chili; sauce: soy, gravy, hot, teriyaki; starch: noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Simmer broth or water with the ginger and the chicken broth for 10 minutes.
2. Add the chicken breast and simmer until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C). Boil any eggs hard, 10 minutes, and peel.
3. Cook the ramen noodles separately to the pack time, then drain.
4. Add the bok choy to the broth for the last 3 minutes, then ladle over the ramen noodles in bowls.

**Lighter tip:** Make the veg half the bowl, use a light hand with the soy and keep the broth the main event.

- [ ] Is it clear the broth is made from broth or water, not soy sauce alone?

### Egg Foo Young with Cabbage

`eggfooyoung` · Asian · 20 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein eggs, veg cabbage, spice ginger, sauce soy sauce, starch jasmine rice.

**Accepts:** protein: egg; veg: cabbage, carrot, greenbeans, mushroom, onion, greens, pepper; spice: ginger, garlic, chili; sauce: soy, gravy, hot, teriyaki; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Sauté the cabbage with the ginger until just tender, then let cool a few minutes.
2. Whisk the eggs (about 2 to 3 per person) and stir in the veg.
3. Cook in a lightly oiled pan in small patties, 2 to 3 minutes a side, until fully set (160°F / 71°C).
4. Serve over the jasmine rice with the soy sauce spooned on top.

**Lighter tip:** Pack the patties with veg, cook them in a little oil and go light on the sauce.

**The same pot also offers:** Eggs & Cabbage Stir-Fry; Bibimbap with Eggs.

### Ground Chicken Lettuce Wraps

`lettucewraps` · Asian · 20 minutes · Light

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ground chicken, veg carrots, spice ginger, sauce hoisin sauce, starch jasmine rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, pork, beef, tofu, shrimp, turkey; veg: greens, carrot, cucumber, mushroom, cabbage, onion, greenbeans; spice: ginger, garlic, chili; sauce: teriyaki, soy, peanut, hot, honey; starch: rice, noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the ground chicken with the ginger in a hot pan, breaking it up, until cooked through (ground chicken 165°F / 74°C).
2. Add the hoisin sauce and stir until glossy.
3. Chop the carrots fine and fold in at the end, or pile on top for crunch.
4. Spoon into crisp lettuce or cabbage leaves and serve with the jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Lettuce in place of the wrap and extra veg in the filling keep this light.

**The same pot also offers:** Glazed Ground Chicken & Carrots Bowl; Ground Chicken Fried Rice.
