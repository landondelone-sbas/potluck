# Recipe pilot review sheet

The first dishes from the Gemini recipe sheets, written for the owner's cooking and food-safety review. Everything below is generated from the engine, so what you read is what the app shows.

- **Plated** dishes are a protein with two sides, each cooked on its own. **One pot** dishes cook together.
- Amounts are for 2 servings and are metric here. The app converts them.
- The steps are rewritten, not copied from the sources. Each dish names its source.
- A temperature in the steps is the USDA minimum. Where a source asked for a different one, the change is noted.
- Set aside from the sheets for now: recipes with no way to fit the five slots, and near-duplicates.
- A step like "Toss the broccoli with oil and salt and roast..." is a generic side method, not the source's.

## Roast X with Y (`roastchicken`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 60 min | **Feel:** Hearty
- **Source:** Sheet "Chicken": Perfect Whole Roast Chicken (How To Roast a Whole Chicken, Garden in the Kitchen) and Crispy Baked Chicken Thighs (Downshiftology).
- **What changed from the source:** Merged two near-duplicate recipes into one that works for pieces or a whole bird, at 425°F for both. The sheet pulled thighs at 170°F for texture. The app says 165°F, the USDA minimum. The higher figure is a cook's choice.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg carrot, onion, broccoli, greenbeans, cauliflower, asparagus, zucchini, celery; spice garlic, paprika, herbs, pepper, smoky, lemon; sauce gravy, mustard, honey, vinaigrette; starch potato, sweetpotato, rice, bread, quinoa
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken thighs (700 g); veg: carrots (250 g); spice: smoked paprika (10 ml); sauce: gravy (120 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** sauce:gravy

**With its own defaults** (Roast Chicken Thighs with Carrots):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Pat the chicken thighs very dry, then rub with oil, salt and the smoked paprika.
2. Roast the chicken thighs on a sheet pan until cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C): about 25 to 45 minutes for pieces (breasts take the least), or about 1 hour 15 minutes for a whole bird. Rest 10 minutes before carving.
3. While it roasts, get the sides going. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the carrots with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
4. Plate the chicken thighs with the carrots and baby potatoes, and spoon the gravy over the top.

**With Chicken breast, Broccoli, Jasmine rice, Garlic** (Roast Chicken Breast with Broccoli):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Pat the chicken breast very dry, then rub with oil, salt and the garlic.
2. Roast the chicken breast on a sheet pan until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C): about 25 to 45 minutes for pieces (breasts take the least), or about 1 hour 15 minutes for a whole bird. Rest 10 minutes before carving.
3. While it roasts, get the sides going. Cook the jasmine rice in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the broccoli with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
4. Plate the chicken breast with the broccoli and jasmine rice, and spoon the gravy over the top.

**Lighter tip:** Roast extra vegetables on the same pan, and keep the skin to a few bites, for a lighter plate.

## Creamy Garlic X with Y (`tuscanchicken`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Italian | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Creamy
- **Source:** Sheet "Chicken": Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken (50 Side Dishes for Chicken, A Couple Cooks).
- **What changed from the source:** Protein and veg made flexible, and the starch is pasta. Sun-dried tomatoes dropped. A lighter-sauce tip replaces the heavy cream.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg greens, tomato, mushroom, asparagus, broccoli; spice garlic, herbs, chili; sauce cream; starch pasta
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken breast (340 g); veg: spinach (100 g); spice: garlic (5 ml); sauce: cream and parmesan (180 ml); starch: linguine (200 g)
- **Signature:** sauce:cream

**With its own defaults** (Creamy Garlic Chicken Breast with Spinach):
1. Season the chicken breast with salt, pepper and the garlic. Sear in a hot oiled skillet until golden and cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then move to a plate.
2. Cook the linguine until tender, and keep a little of its water.
3. Lower the heat and cook the spinach in the same pan for a minute. Pour in a splash of broth and the cream and parmesan, and simmer until lightly thickened.
4. Return the chicken breast and toss in the linguine, loosening with the cooking water if needed. Serve right away.

**With Chicken thighs, Mushrooms, Penne, Alfredo** (Creamy Garlic Chicken Thighs with Mushrooms):
1. Season the chicken thighs with salt, pepper and the garlic. Sear in a hot oiled skillet until golden and cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C), then move to a plate.
2. Cook the penne until tender, and keep a little of its water.
3. Lower the heat and cook the mushrooms in the same pan for a minute. Pour in a splash of broth and the alfredo, and simmer until lightly thickened.
4. Return the chicken thighs and toss in the penne, loosening with the cooking water if needed. Serve right away.

**Lighter tip:** Use milk or half-and-half instead of cream, and add extra spinach.

## Skillet X with Y (`porkchops`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 25 min | **Feel:** Quick
- **Source:** Sheet "Pork": The Best Damn Pan-Seared Pork Chops (Recipe Teacher) and Juicy Skillet Pork Chops with Pan Sauce (Inspired Taste).
- **What changed from the source:** Merged the two. Both sources pull chops at 140°F and let the rest carry them to 145°F. The app says cook to 145°F and rest 3 minutes, the USDA wording. The sources disagree about tenting with foil, and the steps say neither.
- **Accepts:** protein pork; veg greenbeans, broccoli, carrot, asparagus, greens, zucchini, corn; spice paprika, garlic, chili, smoky, pepper; sauce honey, mustard, gravy, vinaigrette; starch potato, rice, sweetpotato, bread, noodles
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: pork chops (450 g); veg: green beans (250 g); spice: smoked paprika (10 ml); sauce: honey mustard (60 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Skillet Pork Chops with Green Beans):
1. Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the green beans with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Pat the pork chops dry and leave out of the fridge for 15 minutes. Rub with the smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
3. Sear the pork chops in a hot, oiled skillet 3 to 4 minutes per side until browned and cooked through (pork chops 145°F / 63°C). Rest 3 minutes.
4. Pour the honey mustard into the hot pan, scrape up the browned bits, and simmer until glossy. Spoon over the pork chops and serve with the green beans and baby potatoes.

**With Pork chops, Carrots, Sweet potato, Hot honey** (Skillet Pork Chops with Carrots):
1. Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cut the sweet potato into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the carrots with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Pat the pork chops dry and leave out of the fridge for 15 minutes. Rub with the smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
3. Sear the pork chops in a hot, oiled skillet 3 to 4 minutes per side until browned and cooked through (pork chops 145°F / 63°C). Rest 3 minutes.
4. Pour the hot honey into the hot pan, scrape up the browned bits, and simmer until glossy. Spoon over the pork chops and serve with the carrots and sweet potato.

**Lighter tip:** Trim the visible fat before cooking and fill half the plate with the vegetable.

## Southwest X with Y (`porktenderloin`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Mexican | **Time:** 35 min | **Feel:** Balanced
- **Source:** Sheet "Pork": Southwest-Spiced Roast Pork Tenderloin (Know Your Pork Cuts, National Pork Board).
- **What changed from the source:** Keeps the 145°F and 3-minute rest. The cook chooses the sides.
- **Accepts:** protein pork; veg pepper, corn, onion, zucchini; spice chili, cumin, garlic, smoky; sauce salsa, hot, honey; starch rice, tortilla, sweetpotato, potato, quinoa
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: pork tenderloin (450 g); veg: bell peppers (250 g); spice: chili powder (10 ml); sauce: salsa (120 ml); starch: jasmine rice (150 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Southwest Pork Tenderloin with Bell Peppers):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cook the jasmine rice in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the bell peppers with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Trim the pork tenderloin, rub with oil, and coat evenly with the chili powder and salt.
3. Roast on a sheet pan 20 to 25 minutes, until cooked through (pork tenderloin 145°F / 63°C). Rest 3 minutes, then slice into medallions.
4. Serve the pork tenderloin with the bell peppers and jasmine rice, and top with the salsa.

**With Pork tenderloin, Corn, Baby potatoes, Cumin** (Southwest Pork Tenderloin with Corn):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Cook the corn in a hot pan, or boil it, until tender and lightly browned.
2. Trim the pork tenderloin, rub with oil, and coat evenly with the cumin and salt.
3. Roast on a sheet pan 20 to 25 minutes, until cooked through (pork tenderloin 145°F / 63°C). Rest 3 minutes, then slice into medallions.
4. Serve the pork tenderloin with the corn and baby potatoes, and top with the salsa.

**Lighter tip:** Pork tenderloin is one of the leanest cuts. Add beans or extra vegetables to stretch it.

## Baked X with Y (`bakedsalmon`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Mediterranean | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Light
- **Source:** Sheet "Salmon": Baked Salmon with Fresh Lemon and Dill (7 Nutrient-Packed Recipes, Prime Women).
- **What changed from the source:** Baked at 425°F for 10 to 12 minutes, not 375°F for 15 to 20, so it shares the oven with the roasted sides. Defaults deliberately differ from Lemon-Herb Salmon (green beans, potatoes, mustard, no lemon) so the two do not compete for the same pot.
- **Accepts:** protein fish; veg asparagus, greenbeans, broccoli, tomato, greens; spice lemon, herbs, garlic, pepper; sauce mustard, vinaigrette; starch potato, rice, couscous
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: salmon (340 g); veg: green beans (250 g); spice: dill and garlic (5 ml); sauce: dijon mustard (30 ml); starch: baby potatoes (150 g)
- **Signature:** protein:fish

**With its own defaults** (Baked Salmon with Green Beans):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Start the sides. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the green beans with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
3. Set the salmon on the lined pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt and the dill and garlic.
4. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and is cooked through (salmon 145°F / 63°C).
5. Serve the salmon with the green beans and baby potatoes, with the dijon mustard spooned over.

**With Salmon, Asparagus, Couscous, Marinara** (Baked Salmon with Asparagus):
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Start the sides. Cook the couscous in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the asparagus with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
3. Set the salmon on the lined pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt and the dill and garlic.
4. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and is cooked through (salmon 145°F / 63°C).
5. Serve the salmon with the asparagus and couscous, with the marinara spooned over.

**Lighter tip:** Fish with a big pile of green vegetables is about as light as dinner gets.

## X and Y Gumbo (`shrimpgumbo`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Cajun | **Time:** 60 min | **Feel:** Hearty
- **Source:** Sheet "Shrimp": Traditional Shrimp and Chicken Okra Gumbo (The Nutrition Source, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health).
- **What changed from the source:** Takes shrimp, chicken or sausage. Okra is the signature, so a pot with okra is pulled toward it.
- **Accepts:** protein shrimp, chicken, sausage; veg pepper, onion, celery, okra, tomato; spice cajun, garlic, herbs, smoky, paprika; sauce tomato, gravy; starch rice
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: shrimp (340 g); veg: okra (400 g); spice: cajun seasoning (10 ml); sauce: crushed tomatoes (400 ml); starch: long-grain rice (150 g)
- **Signature:** veg:okra

**With its own defaults** (Shrimp and Okra Gumbo):
1. Cook the long-grain rice following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. In a large pot, soften the okra in oil for about 5 minutes, then stir in the cajun seasoning for a minute.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes and 3 cups of broth. Simmer, covered, for about 25 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
4. Add the shrimp and cook until cooked through (shrimp pink and opaque). Serve over the long-grain rice.

**With Italian sausage, Red onion, Brown rice, Cajun seasoning** (Italian Sausage and Red Onion Gumbo):
1. Cook the brown rice following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. In a large pot, soften the red onion in oil for about 5 minutes, then stir in the cajun seasoning for a minute.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes and 3 cups of broth. Simmer, covered, for about 25 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
4. Add the italian sausage and cook until cooked through (italian sausage 165°F / 74°C). Serve over the brown rice.

**Lighter tip:** Okra thickens the gumbo, so you can skip the heavy roux and keep it lighter.

- **Source:** Sheet "Other Proteins": Spicy Vegetarian Mapo Tofu with Shiitakes (The Nutrition Source, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health).

## Cheesy X Skillet with Y (`cheesybeefpotatoes`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 40 min | **Feel:** Hearty
- **Source:** Sheet "Beef": One-Skillet Cheesy Ground Beef and Potatoes (The Kitchn).
- **What changed from the source:** The meat is beef, ground or sausage. The source says 160°F for ground beef, as does the app.
- **Accepts:** protein beef, ground, sausage; veg onion, pepper, broccoli, greenbeans, corn, mushroom; spice paprika, garlic, smoky, pepper, herbs; sauce cream; starch potato, sweetpotato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: ground beef (450 g); veg: onions (150 g); spice: smoked paprika (5 ml); sauce: cheddar cheese (180 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** protein:ground

**With its own defaults** (Cheesy Ground Beef Skillet with Onions):
1. Dice the baby potatoes. Cook in an oiled skillet, covered, stirring now and then, until golden and tender, about 10 to 12 minutes. Move to a plate.
2. Cook the ground beef with the onions in the same pan, breaking up the meat, until browned and cooked through (ground beef 160°F / 71°C). Drain off extra fat.
3. Stir in the smoked paprika for a minute, then return the baby potatoes.
4. Top with the cheddar cheese, cover, and cook until melted and bubbly, 2 to 3 minutes.

**With Italian sausage, Broccoli, Sweet potato, Alfredo** (Cheesy Italian Sausage Skillet with Broccoli):
1. Dice the sweet potato. Cook in an oiled skillet, covered, stirring now and then, until golden and tender, about 10 to 12 minutes. Move to a plate.

3. Stir in the smoked paprika for a minute, then return the sweet potato.
4. Top with the alfredo, cover, and cook until melted and bubbly, 2 to 3 minutes.

**Lighter tip:** Use lean meat, drain the fat, and go light on the cheese. Stir in extra vegetables.
