## Rosemary Roast X with Y (`lambroast`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 105 min | **Feel:** Rich
- **Source:** RecipeTin Eats: Classic Sunday Roasts, Classic Roast Lamb Leg with Gravy.
- **What changed from the source:** The source pulls the leg at 135°F for medium-rare. The app gives the USDA 145°F / 63°C. The potatoes and vegetable are cooked as sides, started 45 minutes before the lamb is done. The gravy is kept.
- **Accepts:** protein lamb; veg greenbeans, carrot, asparagus, broccoli, onion; spice herbs, garlic, pepper; sauce gravy; starch potato, sweetpotato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: leg of lamb (900 g); veg: green beans (250 g); spice: rosemary (5 ml); sauce: beef broth (240 ml); starch: potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Rosemary Roast Leg Of Lamb with Green Beans):
1. Heat the oven to 400°F (204°C). Cut deep slits all over the leg of lamb and push in slivers of garlic and small clusters of the rosemary. Rub with oil, salt and pepper.
2. Set the leg of lamb on a rack in a roasting pan.
3. Roast 15 minutes, then turn the oven down to 350°F (177°C) and roast about 1 hour more, until the thickest part is cooked through (leg of lamb 145°F / 63°C). Check with a thermometer.
4. About 45 minutes before the lamb is done, start the sides. Cut the potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the green beans with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
5. Rest the leg of lamb 15 to 20 minutes under foil.
6. Whisk 2 tablespoons of flour into the pan drippings over medium heat for a minute, pour in the beef broth, scrape up the browned bits and simmer 3 to 4 minutes, until thick. Carve across the grain and serve with the potatoes, the green beans and the gravy.

**With Lamb, Carrots, Sweet potatoes** (Rosemary Roast Lamb with Carrots):

## Crispy-Edge Fried X with Y (`friedeggs`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 10 min | **Feel:** Quick
- **Source:** Food Network: 10 Mistakes Everyone Makes Cooking Eggs, Mistake 4: Frying Technique.
- **What changed from the source:** Heat is high to start and then low, as in the source. The source leaves the yolk liquid. The app gives the safer firm-yolk target (160°F / 71°C) and says a runny yolk is your choice and carries more risk.

- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: eggs (2); veg: spinach (150 g); spice: black pepper (2 ml); sauce: hot sauce (10 ml); starch: sourdough (2)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Crispy-Edge Fried Eggs with Spinach):
1. Start the sides first. Warm or toast the sourdough. Wilt the spinach in a hot pan with a little oil until just soft.
2. Heat a generous spoonful of oil or butter in a skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Slip the eggs in, then turn the heat down to low right away.
3. Tilt the pan and spoon the hot oil over the whites near the yolks. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, until the whites are opaque with golden, crisp edges.
4. For the safest finish, keep cooking until the yolks are firm: the target is cooked through (eggs set and firm, 160°F / 71°C). A runny yolk is your choice and carries more risk.
5. Season with the black pepper, set the eggs on the sourdough with the spinach, and add the hot sauce.

2. Heat a generous spoonful of oil or butter in a skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Slip the eggs in, then turn the heat down to low right away.
3. Tilt the pan and spoon the hot oil over the whites near the yolks. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, until the whites are opaque with golden, crisp edges.

## Golden X Scramble with Y (`tofuscramble`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 15 min | **Feel:** Quick
- **Source:** Frugal Vegan by Nicole Davis and Celine Steen: Tofu Scrambled Eggs.
- **What changed from the source:** The nutritional yeast and plant milk are chef adds. The vegetable and the toast are cooked as sides, and the spice is a slot (turmeric is the default for color).
- **Accepts:** protein tofu; veg greens, tomato, pepper, mushroom, onion; spice curry, garlic, paprika, pepper; sauce hot, salsa, soy; starch bread, tortilla, potato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: tofu (400 g); veg: spinach (150 g); spice: turmeric (3 ml); sauce: salsa (30 ml); starch: whole-grain toast (2)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Golden Tofu Scramble with Spinach):
1. Start the sides first. Warm or toast the whole-grain toast. Wilt the spinach in a hot pan with a little oil until just soft.
2. Crumble in the pressed tofu with your hands. Sprinkle with the turmeric, a spoonful of nutritional yeast and a pinch of salt, and add a splash of plant milk.
3. Stir gently 6 to 8 minutes, until heated through and the moisture has cooked off into a soft scramble.
4. Serve with the spinach, the whole-grain toast and the salsa.

**With Tofu, Bell peppers, Tortillas** (Golden Tofu Scramble with Bell Peppers):
1. Start the sides first. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan until soft and pliable. Toss the bell peppers with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Crumble in the pressed tofu with your hands. Sprinkle with the turmeric, a spoonful of nutritional yeast and a pinch of salt, and add a splash of plant milk.
3. Stir gently 6 to 8 minutes, until heated through and the moisture has cooked off into a soft scramble.
4. Serve with the bell peppers, the tortillas and the salsa.

**Lighter tip:** Add extra vegetables and keep to one slice of toast.

## Crispy X Larb with Y (`tofularb`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Thai | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Zesty
- **Source:** The Complete Veggie Air Fryer Cookbook (referenced in Effortless Meals, Livingston Public Library): Vibrant Tofu Larb.
- **What changed from the source:** The source air-fries the tofu. The app pan-fries it, so no air fryer is needed. The cabbage and cucumber go on the side. The toasted rice powder is kept.
- **Accepts:** protein tofu; veg cucumber, cabbage, onion, greens, tomato; spice herbs, chili, lemon, garlic; sauce soy, vinaigrette, hot; starch rice
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: tofu (400 g); veg: cabbage (250 g); spice: mint (5 ml); sauce: soy sauce (30 ml); starch: sticky rice (150 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Crispy Tofu Larb with Cabbage):
1. Start the sides first. Cook the sticky rice in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Shred the cabbage and sauté it in a hot pan until just tender.
2. Press and crumble the tofu, then toss with a spoonful of soy sauce and a little oil. Pan-fry in a large skillet over medium-high heat 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and crisp at the edges.
3. Toast a spoonful of uncooked rice in a dry pan 5 minutes, until golden, and grind it to a coarse powder.
4. Whisk lime juice, the soy sauce, a pinch of sugar and chili flakes. Toss with the warm tofu, thinly sliced shallot, the toasted rice and the mint.
5. Serve with the cabbage and the sticky rice.

**With Tofu, Cucumber, Jasmine rice** (Crispy Tofu Larb with Cucumber):
1. Start the sides first. Cook the jasmine rice in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Slice the cucumber and dress it with a little salt, oil and lemon.
2. Press and crumble the tofu, then toss with a spoonful of soy sauce and a little oil. Pan-fry in a large skillet over medium-high heat 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and crisp at the edges.
3. Toast a spoonful of uncooked rice in a dry pan 5 minutes, until golden, and grind it to a coarse powder.
4. Whisk lime juice, the soy sauce, a pinch of sugar and chili flakes. Toss with the warm tofu, thinly sliced shallot, the toasted rice and the mint.
5. Serve with the cucumber and the jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Heap on the cabbage and cucumber so the tofu is only part of the plate.
