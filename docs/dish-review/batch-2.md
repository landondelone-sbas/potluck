The food-safety numbers and their sources are in batch 1. The dish files in `packages/dishes/data` are the source of truth, and this sheet does not update itself.

Differences from batch 1 worth knowing:

- Steps that say "cooked through (…)" get their temperatures from what is in the pot (see decision record 0009), so the sheet shows them for the chef pick only. Try a different protein to see another phrase.
- Two dishes use dried red kidney beans (red beans and rice) and carry the soak-and-boil warning. Canned beans skip it.
- Shakshuka and chilaquiles give 160°F / 71°C for firm yolks. Runny yolks are a personal choice and the card does not offer them.

## The thirteen dishes


### Chicken Breast Quesadilla

`quesadilla` · Mexican · 20 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg bell peppers, spice cumin, sauce shredded cheese, starch flour tortillas.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, beef, pork, shrimp, beans, tofu, turkey; veg: pepper, onion, tomato, corn, greens, mushroom, avocado; spice: cumin, chili, paprika, garlic, smoky; sauce: cream, salsa, hot, ranch; starch: tortilla.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the chicken breast with the cumin until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then move to a clean plate.
2. Sauté the bell peppers until soft.
3. Fill the flour tortillas with the chicken breast, the bell peppers and the shredded cheese, fold, and cook in a dry pan 2 to 3 minutes a side, until crisp and melted.
4. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa or lime.

**Lighter tip:** Use less cheese, add extra veg, and choose corn tortillas or a whole-wheat wrap.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Breast Fajita Skillet; Chicken Breast Enchilada Bake.

### Chilaquiles with Eggs

`chilaquiles` · Mexican · 20 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein eggs, veg red onion, spice chili powder, sauce salsa, starch corn tortillas.

**Accepts:** protein: egg; veg: onion, pepper, tomato, corn, greens, avocado; spice: chili, cumin, garlic, smoky, paprika; sauce: salsa, hot, cream; starch: tortilla.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cut the corn tortillas into wedges and crisp them in a hot pan with a little oil, or bake until crunchy.
2. Sauté the red onion with the chili powder, then add the salsa and simmer 3 minutes.
3. Stir the corn tortillas into the sauce just to coat, and fry or poach the eggs until the whites are fully set (160°F / 71°C for firm yolks).
4. Top with the eggs and serve right away, so the chips stay a little crisp.

**Lighter tip:** Bake the tortilla wedges instead of frying them, and top with extra veg and avocado.

**The same pot also offers:** Eggs Tacos; Red Onion & Egg Scramble.

- [ ] Chilaquiles sits next to tacos and the scramble for an egg and tortilla pot. Is that fine?

### Chicken Breast Fajita Skillet

`fajita` · Mexican · 25 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg bell peppers, spice chili powder, sauce salsa, starch flour tortillas.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, beef, pork, shrimp, tofu; veg: pepper, onion, mushroom, zucchini, corn; spice: cumin, chili, paprika, smoky, garlic; sauce: salsa, hot, cream, ranch; starch: tortilla.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Slice the chicken breast thin, season with the chili powder and sear in a very hot pan until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then move to a clean plate.
2. Cook the bell peppers in the same pan until charred at the edges.
3. Return the chicken breast, toss with the salsa and warm through.
4. Serve with the flour tortillas.

**Lighter tip:** Pile on the peppers and onions, and go easy on the sour cream and cheese.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Breast Tacos; Chicken Breast Quesadilla.

### Chicken Breast Enchilada Bake

`enchilada` · Mexican · 40 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg corn, spice cumin, sauce enchilada sauce and cheese, starch corn tortillas.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, beans, turkey, beef, pork, tofu; veg: pepper, onion, corn, tomato, greens, mushroom, zucchini; spice: cumin, chili, garlic, paprika, smoky; sauce: salsa, cream, hot; starch: tortilla.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook the chicken breast with the cumin until browned, then add the corn and cook until soft.
2. Stir in half the enchilada sauce and cheese, then roll or layer the mixture in the corn tortillas in a baking dish.
3. Pour over the rest of the enchilada sauce and cheese, cover and bake 20 to 25 minutes, until bubbling and at least 165°F / 74°C in the center.
4. Rest 5 minutes and serve with something fresh, like cabbage or avocado.

**Lighter tip:** Fill it with extra veg and beans, and use half the cheese.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Breast Quesadilla; Chicken Breast Tacos.

### Chicken Breast Tortilla Soup

`tortillasoup` · Mexican · 35 minutes · Cozy

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg corn, spice chili powder, sauce chicken broth, starch corn tortillas.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, beans, turkey, shrimp, tofu, pork; veg: tomato, corn, pepper, onion, greens; spice: cumin, chili, garlic, smoky, paprika; sauce: gravy, salsa, tomato; starch: tortilla.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Soften the corn with the chili powder in a pot with a little oil.
2. Add the chicken broth and enough water to cover, then the chicken breast, and simmer 20 minutes, until any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside. Add any shrimp for the last 3 minutes, until pink and opaque.
3. Cut the corn tortillas into strips and crisp them in a dry pan or a hot oven.
4. Ladle the soup over the strips and top with avocado, lime or cilantro.

**Lighter tip:** Bake the tortilla strips, load up on veg and keep the cheese and sour cream light.

### Bell Peppers Shakshuka

`shakshuka` · Mediterranean · 25 minutes · Easy

**Cooked from this pot:** protein eggs, veg bell peppers, spice cumin, sauce crushed tomatoes, starch pita.

**Accepts:** protein: egg; veg: tomato, pepper, onion, greens, mushroom, zucchini; spice: cumin, paprika, garlic, smoky; sauce: tomato, hot; starch: bread, couscous, potato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Soften the bell peppers with the cumin in a wide pan with a little oil.
2. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes, until thick.
3. Make wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5 to 8 minutes, until the whites are fully set (160°F / 71°C for firm yolks).
4. Serve straight from the pan with the pita for scooping.

**Lighter tip:** Pile in extra veg and scoop with bread instead of adding cheese.

### Chicken Breast Pita Wrap

### Stuffed Peppers with Ground Turkey

`stuffedpeppers` · Mediterranean · 50 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ground turkey, veg bell peppers, spice italian herbs, sauce tomato sauce, starch brown rice.

**Accepts:** protein: ground, sausage, beans; veg: pepper; spice: herbs, garlic, paprika, cumin, pepper, cinnamon; sauce: tomato, gravy; starch: rice, couscous, quinoa.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and cook the brown rice until just tender.
2. Brown the ground turkey with the italian herbs, then mix in the brown rice and half the tomato sauce.
3. Cut the tops off the bell peppers, scoop out the seeds and stuff them. Stand them in a dish, spoon over the rest of the tomato sauce, cover and bake 35 to 40 minutes, until the filling is at least 165°F / 74°C.
4. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

**Lighter tip:** Use lean turkey or beans, plenty of rice or quinoa, and skip the cheese topping.

**The same pot also offers:** Ground Turkey & Bell Peppers Cacciatore.

### Chicken Thighs & Carrots Tagine

`tagine` · Mediterranean · 50 minutes · Slow

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken thighs, veg carrots, spice cinnamon, sauce chicken broth, starch couscous.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, lamb, beans, beef, turkey; veg: carrot, onion, tomato, zucchini, cauliflower, greens, pepper; spice: cumin, cinnamon, paprika, ginger, garlic, chili; sauce: tomato, gravy, honey; starch: couscous, rice, bread, quinoa.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Brown the chicken thighs in a heavy pot, then set aside on a clean plate.
2. Soften the carrots with the cinnamon, then add the chicken broth and enough water or stock to half-cover.
3. Return the chicken thighs, cover and simmer gently 30 to 40 minutes, until tender and any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.
4. Serve over the couscous, with something fresh, like lemon or herbs.

**Lighter tip:** Beans can replace half the meat, and the veg and spices carry most of the flavor.

### Andouille Sausage & Bell Peppers Jambalaya

`jambalaya` · Cajun · 45 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein andouille sausage, veg bell peppers, spice cajun seasoning, sauce tomato sauce, starch long-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: sausage, chicken, shrimp, pork, turkey; veg: pepper, onion, celery, tomato, greens, corn; spice: cajun, paprika, chili, garlic, smoky, pepper; sauce: tomato, hot, gravy; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Brown the andouille sausage, leaving out any shrimp, in a heavy pot, then set aside on a clean plate.
2. Soften the bell peppers with the cajun seasoning, then stir in the long-grain rice to coat.
3. Add the tomato sauce and enough stock or water to cover the rice by a knuckle, return the meat, cover and simmer on low until the rice is tender, about 20 minutes.
4. Stir in any shrimp for the last 5 minutes, until pink and opaque, and check that the other meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.

**Lighter tip:** Use less sausage and more peppers, onion and celery, and brown rice if you have the time.

**The same pot also offers:** Andouille Sausage & Bell Peppers Cajun Skillet.

### Cajun Red Beans & Celery with Rice

`redbeans` · Cajun · 45 minutes · Slow

**Cooked from this pot:** protein red beans, veg celery, spice cajun seasoning, sauce hot sauce, starch long-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: beans, sausage, pork; veg: onion, pepper, celery, tomato, greens; spice: cajun, paprika, garlic, smoky, chili, pepper, herbs; sauce: hot, gravy, tomato; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Soften the celery with the cajun seasoning in a heavy pot with a little oil.
2. Add the red beans, brown any sausage or pork first, the hot sauce and enough water or stock to cover. Dried red kidney beans need soaking, then a hard boil of at least 10 minutes, first; canned beans only need draining.
3. Simmer 30 to 45 minutes, mashing some beans against the pot to thicken, until any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.
4. Serve over the long-grain rice.

**Lighter tip:** Use more beans than sausage and plenty of celery and peppers to keep it lighter and less salty.

### Blackened Tilapia with Corn

`blackened` · Cajun · 25 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein tilapia, veg corn, spice cajun seasoning, sauce hot sauce, starch long-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: fish, chicken, shrimp, pork, turkey; veg: pepper, onion, corn, greens, tomato, zucchini, greenbeans; spice: cajun, paprika, smoky, chili, garlic, pepper; sauce: hot, ranch, tomato, mustard; starch: rice, potato, bread.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Pat the tilapia dry, coat well with the cajun seasoning and sear in a very hot, oiled pan until cooked through (tilapia 145°F / 63°C), turning once.
2. Cook the corn in the same pan until tender and lightly charred.
3. Serve with the long-grain rice and the hot sauce.

**Lighter tip:** Fish or shrimp with a pile of veg and a small portion of rice keeps this light, and the seasoning does the work.

**The same pot also offers:** Tilapia & Corn Cajun Skillet.
