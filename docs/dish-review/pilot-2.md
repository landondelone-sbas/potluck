# Recipe pilot, second batch: review sheet

Thirteen more dishes from the Gemini recipe sheets, for the owner's cooking and food-safety review. Generated from the engine, so what you read is what the app shows. See [the first batch](pilot.md) for how to read it.

- **Plated** dishes are a protein with two sides, each cooked on its own. **One pot** dishes cook together.
- Amounts are for 2 servings and metric here. The app converts them.
- Steps are rewritten, not copied. A step such as "Toss the broccoli with oil and salt and roast..." is a generic side method.
- Temperatures are the USDA minimums. Where a source asked for a different one, the change is noted.
- Steak dishes always carry the line that a lower doneness is the cook's choice and carries more risk.

## Lemon-Herb Grilled X with Y (`lemonrosemary`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Mediterranean | **Time:** 40 min | **Feel:** Light
- **Source:** Sheet "Chicken": Grilled Lemon Rosemary Chicken Breast (Heart Healthy Recipe Guide, HFSA, p. 26).
- **What changed from the source:** Made the grill pan or a skillet work as well as an outdoor grill. The source rests the chicken tented in foil. The steps just say rest. The marinade is a quick toss, not a separate dish.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg asparagus, zucchini, greenbeans, tomato, pepper, greens, broccoli; spice lemon, herbs, garlic, pepper; sauce vinaigrette, mustard, honey; starch rice, quinoa, couscous, potato, bread
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken breast (450 g); veg: zucchini (250 g); spice: lemon and rosemary (10 ml); sauce: olive oil and lemon (30 ml); starch: couscous (150 g)
- **Signature:** spice:lemon

**With its own defaults** (Lemon-Herb Grilled Chicken Breast with Zucchini):
1. Toss the chicken breast with oil, a squeeze of lemon, salt and the lemon and rosemary. Leave to marinate 20 minutes.
2. Start the sides while the chicken breast marinates. Cook the couscous in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the zucchini with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
3. Grill or pan-sear the chicken breast over medium-high heat, 6 to 8 minutes per side, until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C).
4. Rest the chicken breast 5 minutes, then slice.
5. Serve the chicken breast with the zucchini and couscous, with the olive oil and lemon drizzled over.

**With Chicken thighs, Asparagus, Quinoa, Garlic** (Lemon-Herb Grilled Chicken Thighs with Asparagus):
1. Toss the chicken thighs with oil, a squeeze of lemon, salt and the garlic. Leave to marinate 20 minutes.
2. Start the sides while the chicken thighs marinates. Cook the quinoa in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the asparagus with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
3. Grill or pan-sear the chicken thighs over medium-high heat, 6 to 8 minutes per side, until cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C).
4. Rest the chicken thighs 5 minutes, then slice.
5. Serve the chicken thighs with the asparagus and quinoa, with the olive oil and lemon drizzled over.

**Lighter tip:** Marinating in lemon and herbs instead of cream or butter keeps lean chicken flavorful and light.

## Pan-Seared X with Y (`butterchicken`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Rich
- **Source:** Sheet "Chicken": Restaurant-Quality Cast-Iron Seared Chicken Breast (The Best Chicken Breast You'll Ever Make, Epicurious 101).
- **What changed from the source:** The source pulls the chicken at 162°F and relies on carryover. The app says 165°F. The butter is the sauce pick and is kept small in the tip.
- **Accepts:** protein chicken; veg asparagus, greenbeans, broccoli, carrot, mushroom, greens, zucchini; spice garlic, herbs, pepper; sauce cream, gravy, mustard; starch potato, rice, bread, noodles, sweetpotato
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken breast (450 g); veg: green beans (250 g); spice: garlic and thyme (5 ml); sauce: butter (30 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Pan-Seared Chicken Breast with Green Beans):
1. Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the green beans with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Bring the chicken breast out of the fridge 15 minutes ahead. Pat very dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Sear the chicken breast in a hot oiled skillet, skin side down if it has skin, 6 to 8 minutes undisturbed until deeply golden.
4. Flip, then add the butter, the garlic and thyme and a splash of water. Spoon the pan juices over the chicken breast for 4 to 5 minutes, until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C). Rest 5 minutes.
5. Slice and serve with the green beans and baby potatoes.

**With Chicken thighs, Mushrooms, Jasmine rice, Alfredo** (Pan-Seared Chicken Thighs with Mushrooms):
1. Start the sides first, since they take the longest. Cook the jasmine rice in water following the package directions, then fluff it with a fork. Toss the mushrooms with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Bring the chicken thighs out of the fridge 15 minutes ahead. Pat very dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Sear the chicken thighs in a hot oiled skillet, skin side down if it has skin, 6 to 8 minutes undisturbed until deeply golden.
4. Flip, then add the alfredo, the garlic and thyme and a splash of water. Spoon the pan juices over the chicken thighs for 4 to 5 minutes, until cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C). Rest 5 minutes.
5. Slice and serve with the mushrooms and jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Skip the skin and use a smaller spoonful of butter. The pan juices still carry plenty of flavor.

## Crispy X with Y (`airfriedchicken`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 35 min | **Feel:** Quick
- **Source:** Sheet "Chicken": Air Fryer Crispy (Un)Fried Chicken (Heart Healthy Recipe Guide, HFSA, p. 22).

- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: chicken tenders (450 g); veg: broccoli (250 g); spice: smoked paprika (5 ml); sauce: ranch (60 ml); starch: sweet potato (450 g)
- **Signature:** none

**With its own defaults** (Crispy Chicken Tenders with Broccoli):
1. Start the sides in the oven. Cut the sweet potato into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the broccoli with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Soak the chicken tenders in buttermilk, or milk with a squeeze of lemon, for 15 minutes.
3. Mix breadcrumbs with a little Parmesan and the smoked paprika. Press the chicken tenders into the crumbs on all sides.
4. Air fry at 390°F (200°C) for 10 to 15 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and cooked through (chicken tenders 165°F / 74°C). Or bake on a rack at 425°F (220°C) for about 20 minutes.
5. Serve the chicken tenders with the broccoli and sweet potato, and the ranch for dipping.

**With Chicken breast, Green beans, Baby potatoes, Hot honey** (Crispy Chicken Breast with Green Beans):
1. Start the sides in the oven. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Toss the green beans with oil and salt and roast at 425°F (220°C) until tender and browned at the edges, about 15 to 25 minutes.
2. Soak the chicken breast in buttermilk, or milk with a squeeze of lemon, for 15 minutes.
3. Mix breadcrumbs with a little Parmesan and the smoked paprika. Press the chicken breast into the crumbs on all sides.
4. Air fry at 390°F (200°C) for 10 to 15 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C). Or bake on a rack at 425°F (220°C) for about 20 minutes.
5. Serve the chicken breast with the green beans and baby potatoes, and the hot honey for dipping.

**Lighter tip:** Air frying or baking gives the crunch with a fraction of the oil of deep frying.

## Oven BBQ X with Y (`ovenribs`)

- **Format:** Plated: protein + 2 sides | **Cuisine:** Comfort | **Time:** 200 min | **Feel:** Slow
- **Source:** Sheet "Pork": Oven-Baked St. Louis Style BBQ Ribs (The Ultimate Guide to Pork Ribs, Smithfield).
- **What changed from the source:** Ribs are done by tenderness, far past the 145°F safety minimum. The steps say both. The dry rub is the spice pick and the BBQ sauce is the sauce pick.
- **Accepts:** protein pork; veg cabbage, corn, greenbeans, carrot, onion, pepper; spice smoky, paprika, garlic, pepper, chili; sauce bbq; starch potato, sweetpotato, bread
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: pork ribs (800 g); veg: cabbage (250 g); spice: smoked paprika (15 ml); sauce: BBQ sauce (120 ml); starch: baby potatoes (450 g)
- **Signature:** sauce:bbq

**With its own defaults** (Oven BBQ Pork Ribs with Cabbage):
1. Heat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Pull off the thin membrane on the back of the pork ribs. Rub all over with the smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Seal the pork ribs tightly in foil with a splash of vinegar. Bake 2 1/2 to 3 hours, until very tender and the meat pulls back from the bone ends. It is safe once cooked through (pork ribs 145°F / 63°C), and ribs are well past that by the time they are tender.
3. About 45 minutes before the end, start the sides. Cut the baby potatoes into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until golden and fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Shred the cabbage and sauté it in a hot pan until just tender.
4. Open the foil and brush the pork ribs with the bbq sauce. Broil 3 to 5 minutes until the glaze bubbles. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.
5. Serve the pork ribs with the cabbage and baby potatoes.

**With Pork ribs, Corn, Sweet potato, BBQ sauce** (Oven BBQ Pork Ribs with Corn):
1. Heat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Pull off the thin membrane on the back of the pork ribs. Rub all over with the smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Seal the pork ribs tightly in foil with a splash of vinegar. Bake 2 1/2 to 3 hours, until very tender and the meat pulls back from the bone ends. It is safe once cooked through (pork ribs 145°F / 63°C), and ribs are well past that by the time they are tender.
3. About 45 minutes before the end, start the sides. Cut the sweet potato into chunks and roast at 425°F (220°C) until fork-tender, about 30 minutes. Cook the corn in a hot pan, or boil it, until tender and lightly browned.
4. Open the foil and brush the pork ribs with the bbq sauce. Broil 3 to 5 minutes until the glaze bubbles. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.
5. Serve the pork ribs with the corn and sweet potato.

**Lighter tip:** Ribs are a treat. Serve a smaller portion with a big pile of slaw or green vegetables.

## Red Curry X with Y (`redcurryshrimp`)

- **Format:** One pot | **Cuisine:** Thai | **Time:** 30 min | **Feel:** Bold
- **Source:** Sheet "Shrimp": Thai Red Curry Shrimp with Vegetables (The Nutrition Source, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health).
- **What changed from the source:** Fish and tofu also fit. Fish sauce, brown sugar, bamboo shoots and Thai basil are dropped to keep to the five slots.
- **Accepts:** protein shrimp, fish, tofu; veg pepper, greens, zucchini, mushroom, carrot, greenbeans; spice curry, ginger, garlic, chili, herbs; sauce coconut; starch rice, noodles
- **Chef defaults and amounts (2 servings):** protein: shrimp (340 g); veg: bell peppers (250 g); spice: red curry paste (30 ml); sauce: coconut milk (400 ml); starch: jasmine rice (150 g)
- **Signature:** protein:shrimp

**With its own defaults** (Red Curry Shrimp with Bell Peppers):
1. Cook the jasmine rice following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. Fry the red curry paste in a spoonful of oil for a minute, until fragrant, then whisk in the coconut milk and a cup of broth.
3. Add the bell peppers and simmer 4 to 5 minutes until tender.
4. Add the shrimp and simmer gently until cooked through (shrimp pink and opaque). Serve over the jasmine rice.

**With Tofu, Zucchini, Rice noodles, Ginger** (Red Curry Tofu with Zucchini):
1. Cook the rice noodles following the package directions and keep it warm.
2. Fry the ginger in a spoonful of oil for a minute, until fragrant, then whisk in the coconut milk and a cup of broth.
3. Add the zucchini and simmer 4 to 5 minutes until tender.
4. Add the tofu and simmer gently until heated through. Serve over the rice noodles.

**Lighter tip:** Light coconut milk and plenty of vegetables keep the curry lighter without losing flavor.
