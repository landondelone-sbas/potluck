These nine dishes needed new cuisines in the engine; see [decision record 0010](../decisions/0010-thai-korean-caribbean.md). Steps that say "cooked through (…)" get their temperatures from what is in the pot (decision record 0009), so the sheet shows them for the chef pick only.

Things to know before you cook:

- The engine has no tags for lemongrass, galangal, kimchi (as a dish), sesame, plantain or scotch bonnet peppers, so these dishes lean on the nearest tags: curry paste for curry, fish sauce and miso as soy, kimchi as cabbage, allspice and scotch bonnet as jerk.
- The Indian curry has the same signatures as the Thai and Island curries, so a coconut curry pot offers all three, with the Indian one first. Is that the order you want?
- Eggs are in the bibimbap only. Pad Thai and the noodle dishes leave them out on purpose.

## The nine dishes


### Chicken Breast & Bell Peppers Coconut Curry

`thaicurry` · Thai · 35 minutes · Creamy

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg bell peppers, spice green curry paste, sauce coconut milk, starch jasmine rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, shrimp, tofu, beef, pork, fish, beans; veg: pepper, greenbeans, zucchini, carrot, mushroom, greens, onion, cauliflower; spice: curry, chili, ginger, garlic, lemon; sauce: coconut, hot, soy; starch: rice, noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the green curry paste in a little oil for a minute, until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken breast and bell peppers and stir for 2 minutes, then pour in the coconut milk and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C).
3. Season with soy or fish sauce and lime to taste, and serve over the jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Use more veg than meat, light coconut milk if you have it, and a smaller portion of rice.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Breast & Bell Peppers Curry; Chicken Breast & Bell Peppers Island Curry.

### Pad Thai with Chicken Breast

`padthai` · Thai · 25 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg carrots, spice lime, sauce peanut sauce, starch rice noodles.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, shrimp, tofu, pork, beef; veg: carrot, greens, onion, pepper, greenbeans, cabbage; spice: chili, garlic, lemon, ginger; sauce: peanut, soy, hot, honey; starch: noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the rice noodles to the pack time, drain and toss with a little oil.
2. Stir-fry the chicken breast in a very hot pan until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then move to a clean plate.
3. Stir-fry the carrots with the lime for 2 minutes, add the rice noodles and the peanut sauce and toss until coated.
4. Return the chicken breast, toss, and serve with lime and chopped peanuts.

**Lighter tip:** Go heavy on the veg, use half the sauce, and add lime for brightness instead of more sugar.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Breast & Carrots Noodle Bowl.

### Spicy Basil Ground Pork

`basilstirfry` · Thai · 20 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein ground pork, veg bell peppers, spice thai basil, sauce soy sauce, starch jasmine rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, pork, beef, tofu, shrimp; veg: pepper, greenbeans, onion, mushroom, zucchini, greens; spice: herbs, chili, garlic, ginger; sauce: soy, hot, honey; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Stir-fry the ground pork in a very hot pan, breaking it up, until cooked through (ground pork 160°F / 71°C).
2. Add the bell peppers and stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Add the soy sauce and the thai basil and toss until fragrant and glossy.
4. Serve over the jasmine rice.

**Lighter tip:** Use more veg and basil than meat, and keep the sauce light.

### Bibimbap with Eggs

`bibimbap` · Korean · 35 minutes · Balanced

**Cooked from this pot:** protein eggs, veg carrots, spice garlic, sauce gochujang, starch short-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: egg, beef, chicken, pork, tofu; veg: carrot, greens, mushroom, zucchini, cabbage, greenbeans, onion; spice: garlic, ginger, chili; sauce: hot, soy, honey; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the short-grain rice and keep it warm.
2. Sauté the carrots one at a time with a little oil and the garlic, until just tender.
3. Cook the eggs until heated through, and fry any eggs until the whites are set (160°F / 71°C for firm yolks).
4. Pile the carrots and eggs over the short-grain rice and stir in the gochujang at the table.

**Lighter tip:** Make veg most of the bowl, use one egg and a small spoon of the chili paste.

**The same pot also offers:** Eggs Fried Rice; Egg Foo Young with Carrots.

### Sliced Beef Bulgogi Bowl

`bulgogi` · Korean · 30 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein sliced beef, veg onions, spice garlic, sauce soy sauce, starch short-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: beef, pork, chicken, tofu; veg: onion, mushroom, carrot, greens, cabbage, zucchini, cucumber; spice: garlic, ginger, chili; sauce: soy, honey, teriyaki, hot; starch: rice, noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Slice the sliced beef thin and toss with the soy sauce and the garlic. Marinate in the fridge for 15 minutes to an hour, and throw away any marinade left over.
2. Cook the sliced beef in a very hot pan in batches until cooked through (sliced beef 145°F / 63°C), then move to a clean plate.
3. Cook the onions in the same pan until tender.
4. Serve over the short-grain rice.

**Lighter tip:** Use extra veg, a little less sauce, and slice the meat very thin so a small portion goes far.

**The same pot also offers:** Sliced Beef & Onions Stir-Fry; Spicy Basil Sliced Beef.

### Spicy Chicken Breast & Kimchi Noodles

`spicynoodles` · Korean · 20 minutes · Quick

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken breast, veg kimchi, spice garlic, sauce gochujang, starch udon noodles.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, pork, tofu, shrimp, beef; veg: cabbage, carrot, greens, onion, greenbeans, mushroom, cucumber; spice: garlic, ginger, chili; sauce: hot, soy, honey; starch: noodles.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Cook the udon noodles to the pack time, drain and rinse.
2. Stir-fry the chicken breast until cooked through (chicken breast 165°F / 74°C), then add the kimchi and the garlic for 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Toss with the gochujang and the udon noodles until coated and glossy.
4. Serve hot, topped with sesame seeds or scallions.

**Lighter tip:** Load it with veg, use a small spoon of the chili paste and add a splash of water to stretch it.

### Jerk Chicken Thighs with Bell Peppers

`jerk` · Caribbean · 40 minutes · Bold

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken thighs, veg bell peppers, spice jerk seasoning, sauce hot sauce, starch coconut rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, pork, fish, shrimp, tofu; veg: pepper, onion, greens, zucchini, corn, tomato, carrot; spice: jerk, chili, garlic, ginger, smoky, herbs, cinnamon; sauce: hot, honey, coconut, bbq; starch: rice, potato, sweetpotato.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Coat the chicken thighs well with the jerk seasoning and leave in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
2. Cook the chicken thighs in a 425°F (220°C) oven, on a grill or in a pan, turning now and then, until cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C). Chicken pieces take 35 to 45 minutes; shrimp and thin cuts take far less.
3. Meanwhile cook the bell peppers until tender and lightly charred.
4. Rest 5 minutes and serve with the coconut rice and the hot sauce.

**Lighter tip:** Make the veg half the plate, choose skinless chicken or fish and keep the rice portion modest.

### Coconut Rice & Beans with Bell Peppers

`riceandbeans` · Caribbean · 40 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein red beans, veg bell peppers, spice thyme, sauce coconut milk, starch long-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: beans, chicken, sausage, pork, tofu; veg: pepper, onion, tomato, greens, carrot, corn; spice: herbs, garlic, chili, ginger, smoky, paprika, jerk, cinnamon; sauce: coconut, hot, tomato; starch: rice.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Soften the bell peppers with the thyme in a pot with a little oil.
2. Add the long-grain rice, the red beans, the coconut milk and enough water to cover the rice by a knuckle. Dried red kidney beans need soaking, then a hard boil of at least 10 minutes, first; canned beans only need draining.
3. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low until the rice is tender, about 20 minutes. Check that any meat is at least 165°F / 74°C inside.
4. Fluff with a fork, rest 5 minutes covered, and finish with lime or fresh herbs.

**Lighter tip:** Use more beans than meat, add plenty of veg and finish with lime instead of extra salt.

**The same pot also offers:** Red Beans & Bell Peppers Island Curry.

**Please check:**

- [ ] Raw chicken or sausage cooks in the rice. Does it reach 165°F by the time the rice is done?
- [ ] The kidney bean warning is long. Is it clear enough when the beans are canned?

### Chicken Thighs & Carrots Island Curry

`caribbeancurry` · Caribbean · 45 minutes · Hearty

**Cooked from this pot:** protein chicken thighs, veg carrots, spice curry powder, sauce coconut milk, starch long-grain rice.

**Accepts:** protein: chicken, pork, beef, beans, tofu; veg: carrot, onion, pepper, tomato, greens, zucchini, cauliflower; spice: curry, garlic, ginger, chili, herbs, cinnamon, jerk; sauce: coconut, hot, tomato; starch: rice, potato, sweetpotato, bread.

**Steps as the app shows them:**

1. Season the chicken thighs with the curry powder and brown in a heavy pot, then set aside on a clean plate.
2. Soften the carrots in the same pot, then add the coconut milk and enough water to half-cover.
3. Return the chicken thighs, cover and simmer 30 to 40 minutes, until tender and cooked through (chicken thighs 165°F / 74°C).
4. Serve over the long-grain rice with lime.

**Lighter tip:** Use more veg than meat, light coconut milk and plenty of thyme and ginger for flavor.

**The same pot also offers:** Chicken Thighs & Carrots Curry; Chicken Thighs & Carrots Coconut Curry.

- [ ] No scotch bonnet or allspice unless the pot has them. Is it distinct enough from the Indian curry?
- [ ] Is 30 to 40 minutes enough for pork or beef to be tender?
